Student investigation

  1. Keep gas and pressure constant. How does increasing viscosity affect lava-flow speed?
  2. Keep viscosity constant. Which combination of gas and pressure produces the most explosive eruption?
  3. Run five low-viscosity eruptions. Describe how the volcano changes.
  4. Reset, then run five high-viscosity, high-gas eruptions. Compare the resulting profile.
  5. Explain why broad shield volcanoes and steep stratovolcanoes develop different shapes.
